Dialga

Dialga is one of the four principle gods, usually identified as the god of time, the future, and change. Historic texts usually depict Dialga as a quadruped dragon with blue scales studded with diamonds and clad in steel plating.

Palkia

Palkia is considered the god of space. Alongside Dialga (the god of time) and Giratina (the god of antimatter), Palkia was created by Arceus, the creator of the Pokémon universe.

Giratina

Though traditionally depicted as the god of death and the afterlife, Giratina is occasionally depicted as the god of war, famine, sickness, natural disasters, and severe misfortune, as these are often causes of death to many.

Mew

Mew is considered special in the Pokémon world due to its unique status as a Mythical Pokémon and its supposed possession of the genetic code of all Pokémon.

Mewtwo

Mewtwo is a highly powerful Pokémon, particularly renowned for its immense psychic abilities. It's considered one of the most formidable creatures in the Pokémon world, possessing the ability to use telekinesis and telepathy.

Darkrai

It's known for its ability to manipulate shadows, including teleporting through them and creating additional shadows for strategic advantage. Darkrai can also trap or teleport others to a Dark Void.

Pokémon is a Japanese media franchise consisting of video games, animated series and films, a trading card game, and other related media. The franchise takes place in a shared universe in which humans co-exist with creatures known as Pokémon, a large variety of species endowed with special powers. The original Pokémon is a role-playing game based around building a small team of monsters to battle other monsters in a quest to become the best. Pokémon are divided into types, such as water and fire, each with different strengths. Battles between them can be likened to the simple hand game rock-paper-scissors.
